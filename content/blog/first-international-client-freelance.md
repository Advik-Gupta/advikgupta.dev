## Lesson 1: Time zones are a feature if you plan them

I work from Dubai (GST, UTC+4) and with India (IST, UTC+5:30). My client was [VERIFY: X hours ahead/behind]. At first the gap felt like a problem. Then I started treating it as an overnight handover:

- **Agree on an overlap window.** We fixed [VERIFY: e.g., 1 hour, three days a week] for calls.
- **Send an end-of-day update.** What I did, what's next, and what I need from them. They woke up to progress, not silence.
- **Write questions so they can be answered asynchronously.** "Option A or B? Here's a screenshot of each" beats "can we hop on a call?"
